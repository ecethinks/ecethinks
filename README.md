I am a research and insights team lead. I work across data science and user research to understand how people use Lovable, where they get stuck, and what they need. My work focuses on how people think, how teams learn, and building the systems that turn evidence into sound decisions. I am experienced in team leading practices including Shape Up, Kanban, retrospectives, hiring, and mentorship.

I am currently the Team Lead of Insights at [Lovable](https://lovable.dev), where we build the infrastructure that helps the company learn faster across quantitative telemetry and qualitative user studies. Previously, I co-founded [Scrintal](https://scrintal.com) (a visual workspace that makes networked thinking visible) and [Gobu](https://gobu.ai) (a source-traceable AI research assistant for academic work), both venture-backed startups based in Stockholm.

Before building software companies, I earned a PhD from Stockholm University (2021), analysing how 30 international organisations adapted to climate change through hand-coded policy corpora and quantitative network analysis.
